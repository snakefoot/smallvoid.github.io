In the spring of 2026 then I tried using AI to help implement article sub-categories in Jekyll, but it failed miserably.

Now tried one more time, and this time AI was able to handle the split in posts-collection and articles-collection.

In hindsight having a dedicated folder for articles was a bad idea, and one should have just used the posts-collection together with categories, and explicitly excluded the News-posts where necessary.

The AI really likes creating solutions that are very close to existing solutions, so one should be very careful diverting from the official path, as one becomes very much alone and stranded.

The sub-categories in Jekyll were implemented by introducing a naming convention for categories, where underscore became the splitter and hyphen was just normal space.

Now the [Articles](/articles/) overview works again with links to all sub-categories, and the individual articles also link back to their sub-category.

The tags will probably never be fully working, and each article is now just displaying them as empty links.

Discovered that all links to Microsoft Knowledge Base articles have become broken, so instead replaced them with Bing search links to avoid being punished by many broken links.
